A Reels strategy for restaurants and cafés: what to film and how often to post

Food is a natural fit for short video. A content plan, rather than random posts, turns the rhythm of the kitchen into loyal guests.

FERATSK DİJİTAL · 15 October 2026 · 4 min read

For restaurants, Instagram is no longer a menu showcase. It's where people decide where to eat next, and Reels is where that decision happens fastest.

What to film

  • Prep moments: dough being stretched, a plate leaving the grill, the sound of the pan. Sound is half of these videos.
  • Signature dish: feature the one or two items that define the place, regularly.
  • The team: the chef and staff give the brand a face.
  • Atmosphere: evening light, full tables, music.
  • What's new: seasonal menus, offers, special days.

Technical tips

Shoot vertical (9:16), show the most appealing shot in the first two seconds and keep videos between 15 and 30 seconds. Natural light is best for food; use warm fill light for evening shoots.

Plan for consistency

Several regular posts a week beat one big content day a month. Filming a month's content in a single day is the most efficient approach for both budget and consistency.

Measure and repeat

Track which videos get saved, shared, or lead to bookings or direction requests. Repeat the formats that work with new dishes.
